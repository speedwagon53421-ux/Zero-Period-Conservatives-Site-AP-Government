# Zero Period Conservatives campaign website

## Open the site
Open `index.html` in Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari. Keep `index.html`, `styles.css`, `script.js`, and the `assets` folder together.

## Candidate photos
The supplied portraits of Travis Hudkins and Lincoln Caton are already included permanently in `assets/travis.jpg` and `assets/lincoln.jpg`. No photo upload controls appear on the website. Keep the `assets` folder alongside `index.html` when sharing or publishing.

## Edit website content
- `index.html` — headings, candidates, values, policy plan, team, and page structure.
- `script.js` — the ten expandable issue descriptions.
- `styles.css` — colors, fonts, spacing, and layout.
- `assets/campaign-logo.png` — original elephant campaign logo extracted from page 1 of the provided PDF.

## Publish
Upload the entire folder to any static hosting service (such as GitHub Pages or Netlify). Keep the asset filenames and folder structure unchanged. The Google Fonts stylesheet needs internet access; the site otherwise works locally.

## Source
Based on `Copy of Campaign Plan- CONSERVATIVES.pdf`, including its party platform, leadership, policy agenda, and original logo. Issue explanations are concise paraphrases of the source; the 100-day agenda and legislative items are campaign proposals, not enacted laws. This is a school campaign project, not an official government website.
